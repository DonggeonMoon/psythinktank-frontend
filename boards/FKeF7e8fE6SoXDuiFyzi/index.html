[Today's Stock] Largest Shareholder Group Holds 82%: Only About 17% of Kolon Global Shares Are Left to Trade

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.18
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Key point of this article: the stake has grown, while shares in the market have shrunk

Kolon Global (003070) is the construction, trading, leisure and renewable energy affiliate of Kolon Group. What stands out this time is its shareholder composition. As of September 21, 2026, disclosed data shows that Kolon and 18 others hold 82.35%. In April 2025, the same group of related parties held 76.22%, so the stake has risen by more than 6 percentage points in about a year and a half.

By the numbers

ItemDetails
Largest shareholder group's stake (Sep 21, 2026)82.35% (Kolon and 18 others)
Previous comparison (Apr 2025)76.22% (Kolon and 14 others)
Treasury shares0.39% (as of Apr 2025)
Recent share price (data as of Oct 12)KRW 8,900
Dividend per shareKRW 400
1-year share price change-2.63%

A simple calculation, subtracting the largest shareholder group and treasury shares, leaves roughly 17%. This is my own estimate, and it includes shares held by institutions such as the National Pension Service. The shares actually free to trade on the market (the float) may be even fewer.

Why did this change happen?

In 2025, Kolon Global absorbed Kolon LSI and MOD through mergers. In a merger, new shares are issued and distributed to the shareholders of the other company, so the group's stake composition may have changed in the process. However, I could not confirm the exact reason for the increase in September 2026 in this research. Please check the exact circumstances in the shareholding change filings on DART, Korea's electronic disclosure system.

What happens when the float is small?

  • Trading can become quiet. According to the data provided, the median annual trading value is around KRW 200 million (KRW 208,137,960), a level that makes buying or selling large amounts difficult.
  • Even small volumes can move the price. When there are few buyers and sellers, the gaps between bid and ask prices tend to widen.
  • The largest shareholder's intentions can affect the share price. Firm control brings management stability, but leaves little room for minority shareholders to voice opinions.

How attractive is the dividend?

Dividing the KRW 400 dividend per share by the KRW 8,900 share price gives about 4.5%. This is called the dividend yield. It is worth comparing with deposit interest rates, but construction profits swing widely with site costs and the housing sales market, so there is no guarantee that the dividend will stay at the same level every year.

Earnings points to watch

  • The company said it would make 2026 the first year of an earnings rebound, and that it would book losses from uncertain sites early, in the fourth quarter of 2025. Such front-loading could ease the earnings burden this year.
  • The key is how much the leisure and renewable energy businesses brought in through the mergers offset swings in the construction cycle.
  • News of cooperation on data center development has emerged, but how much it will contribute to actual revenue and profit has yet to be confirmed.
  • Third-quarter results usually come out in mid-November, when we can check whether profitability in the construction division is recovering.

Summary

With its share price nearly unchanged from a year ago (-2.63%), Kolon Global's governance has actually become firmer. It has the advantages of a dividend in the 4% range and the potential for an earnings rebound, but investors must be aware that its float is small and its trading value is low. I suggest watching next quarter's earnings and shareholding change filings together.
