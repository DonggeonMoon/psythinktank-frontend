[Today's Stock] Half-Year Net Income Up 26x, Yet the Stock Sits Near Its 52-Week Low: GMO Product Platform

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.27
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Earnings Turned Profitable, but the Share Price Is Lower Than a Year Ago

GMO Product Platform (3695), listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth market, is a subsidiary of GMO Internet Group. It started out in internet market research and has since expanded into advertising and AI services. What stands out about this stock is that earnings and share price are moving in opposite directions.

Compared with the high at the start of the year, the current price is about 35% lower, and it is not far from the 52-week low.

First-Half Results: The Return to Profit Is the Key

  • Revenue: About 3.72 billion yen, up 17.4% year on year. The advertising business is reported to have driven the growth.
  • Ordinary profit: About 520 million yen, up roughly 489% from the same period a year earlier.
  • Interim net income: About 290 million yen, versus about 10 million yen a year earlier.
  • Full-year outlook: Revised upward in the August announcement; the company's full-year revenue forecast is about 7.09 billion yen.

For reference, full-year 2025 revenue was about 6.82 billion yen. The first half of this year alone has already passed half of last year's full-year revenue.

Why Isn't the Stock Gaining Strength? (Speculation)

What follows is speculation, not confirmed fact.

  • The market cap is small and trading is thin. On some days daily volume stays in the tens of thousands of shares, so even small orders can move the price easily.
  • The largest shareholder holds a big stake. GMO Internet Group owns about 65–67%, which leaves few shares actually trading in the market (the free float). This structure makes trading even thinner.
  • There are concerns about financial strength. Some data services assess that profitability and the equity ratio have weakened over the past three years while debt has increased. The current equity ratio is about 25.8%, and ROE (return on equity) is about 6.8%.

The Dividend Is Attractive, but There Are Caveats

Based on the provided data, the dividend is about 70.9 yen per share, a yield of roughly 4.9%. As net income has grown, so has the capacity to pay dividends. However, since dividends are tied to profit, they could shrink if second-half results weaken. Part of the reason the yield looks high is that the share price has fallen.

Next Checkpoints

  • Q3 results scheduled for November 12: The first test of whether the first-half profit improvement continues.
  • Whether advertising growth holds and margins rise.
  • Whether debt and the equity ratio stabilize.
  • Whether new AI-related services translate into actual revenue.

Summary

Profit has grown sharply and the PER is around 15x, yet the share price is lower than a year ago. Whether this gap narrows depends on Q3 results and improvement in financial indicators. It is also worth remembering that this is a thinly traded small-cap stock.
