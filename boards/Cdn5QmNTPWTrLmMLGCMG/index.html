[Today's Stock] Baking Supply Shop cotta (3359): Net Profit Forecast Up 43% at Around 8x P/E — November Results Are the Key

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.28
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Core Theme: The Share Price Is Flat, but Earnings Are Growing

cotta (3359), listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange, sells baking and confectionery ingredients and packaging online. The recent share price is around 485 yen (as of October 23), about 8% lower than a year ago. Meanwhile, earnings for the fiscal year ending this September are expected to rise sharply. This gap between share price and earnings is the theme of this article.

Note that the share prices in this article are the most recent values I could confirm (485 yen on October 23, 492 yen on October 9). I could not confirm the closing price for October 28.

What Kind of Company Is It?

  • It sells baking and confectionery ingredients and related goods to consumers, bakeries, and bento shops, centered on its online site "cotta." It also accepts fax and phone orders.
  • Through a subsidiary, it processes, manufactures, and sells baking and confectionery ingredients.
  • It also runs an internet media business, such as affiliate advertising. In Yamaguchi Prefecture, it also operates a wholesale (B2B) business for confectionery and bakery products.

Ordinary profit is operating profit plus non-operating items such as interest. The company forecasts operating profit of about 990 million yen and net profit of about 630 million yen. Since last year's net profit was about 440 million yen, net profit would rise about 43% if the forecast holds.

  • Through the third quarter, ordinary profit had reached about 84.6% of the full-year forecast. Barring a major incident in the fourth quarter, hitting the target looks likely. This is an estimate, not a certainty.
  • Sales had reached about 78% of the full-year forecast.
  • The company's forecast and the market consensus (the average analyst forecast; ordinary profit of about 1 billion yen as of August) are nearly the same.

How Attractive Is the Valuation?

  • The price-to-earnings ratio (P/E, which shows how many times earnings the share price is) is about 8x based on the company forecast (a quote-site value as of October 9).
  • The price-to-book ratio (P/B, which shows how many times net assets the share price is) is about 1.08x. Book value per share (BPS) is about 455 yen.
  • The dividend is 10 yen per share, a yield of roughly 2% at 485 yen.
  • The median annual trading value is about 13.2 million yen. Daily trading is very thin, so prices can move sharply when buying or selling.

Shareholder Structure

Major shareholders are a mix of individuals and companies. As of October 1, Akiko Kurosu holds the most at about 10.58%, followed by Takahiro Sato at 5.3%, Fuji Oil at 4.95%, Shimojima at 4.54%, and Seiichi Sato at 3.32%. There was no change in holdings compared with September 20. The national pension fund holds no shares.

What to Watch Next

  • Full-year results: Results for the fiscal year ending September 2026 are expected in mid-November. Last year they came on November 14, and some sources indicated November 13 for this year.
  • Next year's forecast: After this year's large profit increase, how the company presents next year's earnings outlook will be important for the share price.
  • Cost pressure: The business is affected by ingredient prices, logistics costs, and the yen exchange rate.
  • Liquidity: With thin trading, volatility can be large around earnings announcements.
