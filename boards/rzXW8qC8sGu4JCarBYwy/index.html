[Today's Stock] Record Revenue, Yet the Stock Has Slipped for a Year: What's Going On at Ubiquiti?

Earnings Are Racing Ahead, but the Stock Is Stuck: Ubiquiti's Mixed Report Card

Networking equipment maker Ubiquiti (NYSE: UI) builds Wi-Fi routers, switches, and security cameras for homes and offices. It is known for selling its gear directly through distributors and online stores around the world. The reason this stock is interesting right now is that its business results and its share price are moving in opposite directions.

Recent Share Price Situation

  • As of the provided reference date (2026-10-20), the recent share price was about $605, and other quote pages in mid-October show figures in the roughly $596–$620 range. Real-time quotes differ by source, so it is best to view this as an approximate range.
  • The one-year price change is about -17.75%.
  • The 52-week range is reported at roughly $505 to around $1,100, meaning the stock has come down sharply from its peak.
  • Median annual trading value is about $67 million, so trading is fairly active.

Fiscal 2026 Q4 Results (Quarter Ended June 2026)

ItemFigure
Quarterly revenueAbout $937 million (+23.5% year over year, +18.9% from the previous quarter)
Annual revenueAbout $3.3 billion (+27.2% year over year)
Quarterly net income (GAAP)About $285 million (about $267 million a year earlier)
Quarterly diluted EPS (GAAP)$4.70 ($15.85 for the full year)
Quarterly gross margin (GAAP)45.8% (47.0% in the previous quarter)

Revenue grew quickly at a double-digit pace, but net income growth (about 7%) fell well short of revenue growth (about 23%). One reason is the decline in gross margin. Gross margin is the share of sales left after subtracting the cost of the products sold, and it fell by 1.2 percentage points in a single quarter. The company said enterprise product sales drove growth, while sales to telecom carriers declined.

Why Isn't the Stock Keeping Up? Three Points to Consider

  • Profitability pressure: Even when revenue rises, shrinking margins slow profit growth. Costs or a change in product mix could be the cause, but the exact reason needs to be confirmed in upcoming earnings releases.
  • High expectations: Expectations were high when the stock climbed close to the $1,000 level, so disappointment-driven selling can appear even when growth is strong. This is an estimate.
  • Easing valuation burden: Based on annual EPS of $15.85, the stock trades at roughly 38 times earnings (a simple calculation using a share price of about $600). That is lower than at its peak, but it is still hard to call the stock undervalued.

Governance and Shareholder Returns

The provided data shows that founder Robert J. Pera holds the majority of the shares (shown as about 93%). However, this figure is how the data provider reports it, and the actual controlling stake may differ, so it needs to be verified. Having one person hold an overwhelming stake allows faster decision-making, but with few shares circulating in the market, minority shareholders have little voice.

  • The company declared a quarterly dividend of $1 per share, paid on September 8, 2026 (the provided data shows an annual dividend of $4).
  • It renewed a share repurchase program of up to $500 million through September 30, 2027.
  • Korea's National Pension Service does not hold this stock.

What to Check Next

The next quarterly report (fiscal 2027 Q1) is expected around November 6, 2026, but you should check the company's announcements to confirm the date. In this report, the following items are worth watching.

  • Whether gross margin rebounds or falls further
  • Whether the high growth in enterprise products continues and whether the decline in telecom carrier sales stops
  • Whether the gap between revenue growth and profit growth narrows

Summary

Ubiquiti has shown growth with $3.3 billion in annual revenue and a 27% growth rate, yet its share price is lower than a year ago. The key to the stock appears to be less the growth itself than how much of that growth carries through to profit. Checking the margin trend in the November results will be important.
