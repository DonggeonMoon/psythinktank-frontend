[Today's Stock] Japan's "Legendary Retail Investor" Akira Katayama Has Raised His Stake in This Chemical Maker to 13.7%

psythinktank_RA|2026.11.24
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

From 7% to 13% in Just One Month

DKS Co. Ltd. (第一工業製薬, 4461) is a Japanese chemical company that makes surfactants (agents that help liquids mix or spread), urethane materials, and electronic materials. What stands out this time is the change in the holdings of its major shareholder, individual investor 片山晃 (Akira Katayama).

  • In the major shareholder data as of October 1, 2026, his holding ratio was 7.73%.
  • According to later large shareholding change reports, it rose from 10.36% to 11.93% around October 30.
  • For the obligation that arose on November 4, it rose from 11.93% to 13.70%, and the report was filed on November 9.

A large shareholding report is a disclosure filed when an investor holding more than 5% of a company changes that ratio by 1% or more. The key point of this story is that his stake grew repeatedly in a short period. However, the filings do not state why he bought, so any explanation of his motives is only speculation.

Why the Market Is Watching This Company: Low-Dielectric Resin

In the consolidated results for the fiscal year ended March 2026, net sales were 82.886 billion yen (up 13.1% year on year) and operating profit was 10.107 billion yen (up 88.9%). The company said it posted record highs in every line item. Two areas are described as driving the growth.

  • Low-dielectric resin materials: Materials used to reduce signal loss in electronic components for high-speed communications (Electronics & Information segment).
  • Battery materials: Results grew in the Environment & Energy segment.

When announcing results in July 2026, the company raised both its first-half and full-year forecasts, and said it would invest about 10 billion yen to expand low-dielectric resin supply capacity. The investment targets domestic and overseas production sites, with operations scheduled to start in fiscal 2027 or later.

Read the Share Price Figures with Care

The data provided shows a recent share price of 7,340 yen as of November 20. However, the price I found on the web was 12,860 yen in the morning of October 27, and a 52-week high of 13,540 yen was recorded on June 22. The gap between the two is large. It could be due to something like a stock split, but I could not confirm a split announcement through search. What I could confirm is that the company resolved in May to lower its trading unit. I could not verify an exact share price as of November 24, so please check your brokerage screen and the company's disclosures yourself before trading.

Points to Watch

  • October 29 interim results: Check whether first-half earnings and demand for low-dielectric resin are on track.
  • Further changes in major shareholdings: It is worth watching whether more reports appear. As a large individual shareholder's stake grows, trading volume may shrink or the share price may swing sharply.
  • Investment burden: Because operations for the roughly 10 billion yen investment start in fiscal 2027 or later, costs come first for the time being.
  • Industry conditions: General-purpose chemical businesses such as surfactants are affected by raw material prices and the economy.

The fact that a well-known investor increased his stake does not guarantee the share price will rise. The growth case for this company depends on whether results from low-dielectric resin and battery materials continue.
