[Today's Stock] China's No.1 Vape Maker RLX Earns 68% of Sales Overseas, So Why Is the Stock Stuck Near $1?

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.05
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

From a Chinese company to a "global company" — so why is the stock lagging?

RLX Technology (NYSE: RLX) is a China-based e-vapor company (e-vapor means products that heat a liquid and produce vapor to inhale). What is interesting is that the center of gravity of its business has already moved outside China. In the second quarter of 2026, overseas business accounted for 68.5% of net revenue. Yet the share price was about $1.70 as of late September, below the lower end of the most recently confirmed 52-week range (about $1.76–$2.84), and the one-year change is about -32.8%. While the business structure is changing, the market does not yet seem to be rewarding it.

Key Q2 2026 results

ItemQ2 2026Note
Net revenueRMB 1,010.5 million (about US$148.9 million)+14.8% year over year
Gross margin35.4%27.5% a year earlier
Non-GAAP operating incomeRMB 149.6 million+28.8% year over year
GAAP net incomeRMB 222.0 million+1.6% year over year
Non-GAAP net incomeRMB 238.8 millionDown from RMB 291.2 million a year earlier

Non-GAAP figures are profits calculated excluding certain items such as stock-based compensation. Revenue, margins and operating income improved, but net income was roughly flat, and on a non-GAAP basis it declined. This means the money earned from operations did not flow straight through to net income, so changes in lower-line items such as financial and other gains and losses also need to be examined.

Point 1. Margins rose sharply

Gross margin rose from 27.5% to 35.4%, an increase of about 8 percentage points. The shift in revenue mix toward overseas is likely a major factor, but how much each cause contributed needs further confirmation from the company's explanation, so this remains an estimate.

Point 2. Acquisition of a 51% stake in a Western European distributor

In July 2026, the company acquired a 51% stake in a Western European distributor of next-generation smoke-free and consumer products, and its results will be reflected from the third quarter. The aim is to widen its offline distribution network and strengthen its business-to-business (B2B) platform capabilities. However, Europe is a region where e-vapor regulation is tightening, so revenue growth and regulatory risk could grow together.

Shareholder structure

  • Wang (Ying Kate): 45.15%
  • Longtian Holding Limited: 15.63%
  • StarryInv Holding Limited: 10.22%
  • Deep Technology Linkage Fund LP: 9.83%
  • BJBJ Ltd: 7.86%

The stated percentages for the top shareholders are identical in the September 20 and October 1 data. The high stake held by the founder side may make decision-making stable, but it is also worth considering that the number of freely traded shares is limited. The National Pension Service (Korea) does not hold the stock.

Things to check

  • With the share price in the low $1–$2 range, volatility is high. The median annual trading value is about $4.6 million, which is small compared with large-cap stocks.
  • The key is whether the expansion of the overseas share and the acquisition effect show up in the numbers from the third quarter onward.
  • We need to watch whether the margin increase is sustainable and whether net income catches up with the operating improvement.
  • Changes in e-vapor regulation in each country can become a variable for earnings at any time.

In summary, RLX is a stock whose growth metrics and profitability are improving, yet whose share price remains near its 52-week low. The question going forward is whether the market is pricing in regulation and weak demand in China too heavily, or has not yet fully priced in the results of the overseas shift. The latest share price is given as of late September because the figure for October 5 could not be confirmed.
