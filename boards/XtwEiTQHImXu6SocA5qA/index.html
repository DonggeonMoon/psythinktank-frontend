[Today's Stock] Revenue Up 84%, Net Profit Down? The Story Behind PKSHA's 50% Rebound from Its Yearly Low

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.29
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Revenue Up 84%, Net Profit Down 2%: Why the Numbers Point in Different Directions

Japanese AI software company PKSHA Technology (3993, Tokyo Stock Exchange) fell to its 2026 low of 2,484 yen on June 26 this year. On October 28, it climbed to around 3,795 yen during trading, setting a new yearly high (previous high: 3,895 yen). That is a gain of roughly 50% from the low. On October 16, the stock also jumped more than 8% in a single day.

Behind this surge are the cumulative third-quarter results for the fiscal year ending in September (October 2025 to June 2026). Revenue and profit metrics are moving in different directions, so they need some interpretation.

Cumulative Third-Quarter Results at a Glance

ItemFigureYear-over-year
RevenueApprox. 28.3 billion yen+84.1%
Business profitApprox. 4.7 billion yen+46.3%
Quarterly profit attributable to owners of the parentApprox. 2.6 billion yen-2.0%

Business profit can be thought of as profit earned from the core business. Core profit rose sharply, yet final net profit edged down. According to the company, the reason is that one-time gains dropped out, such as the gain on stock sales booked the previous year. In other words, one reading is that the core business is growing and the decline in net profit largely reflects a high comparison base.

What's Behind the Revenue Jump: Acquiring a Staffing Platform Company

The consolidation of Circulation, a company that became a subsidiary, contributed heavily to the large revenue increase. As a result, revenue in the AI Powered Worker business reached roughly 10 billion yen, up more than 600% year over year. This segment aims to combine professional staffing matching with AI.

  • AI SaaS business: Subscription-based AI software such as enterprise chatbots and call center automation. Up about 31.7% year over year
  • AI Research & Solution business: Custom AI development for client companies. Up about 31.4% year over year
  • AI Powered Worker business: Surged due to the acquisition effect

Therefore, the 84% revenue increase should not be read entirely as organic growth of the existing AI businesses. It is worth separating the portion added by the acquisition from the growth of the existing businesses (around 30%).

The Company's Targets for This Year and the Odds of Hitting Them

The company kept its full-year guidance (the company's own forecast) for the fiscal year ending in September unchanged at the third-quarter stage.

  • Revenue of 35 billion yen (up about 60.8% from the previous period)
  • Business profit of 5 billion yen
  • Net profit attributable to owners of the parent of about 2.85 billion to 2.9 billion yen

Since cumulative third-quarter business profit is already 4.7 billion yen, the full-year target of 5 billion yen would be reached with just 300 million yen more in the fourth quarter. However, it has not been confirmed whether costs will be booked all at once in the fourth quarter, so this cannot be stated with certainty. Net profit is also close to the full-year target at a cumulative 2.6 billion yen.

Points to Note on the Share Price and Shareholder Structure

  • Share price: Closed at 3,730 yen on October 27, and around 3,795 yen during trading on the 28th. Based on the provided data (October 23), it was still about 7.6% below the level a year earlier.
  • Dividend: 10 yen per year. Calculated at 3,795 yen, the dividend yield is about 0.26%, making this a stock that leans more toward growth than dividends.
  • Largest shareholder: Founder and CEO Katsuya Uenoyama holds 29.6%, so management and shareholder interests are closely aligned. Toyota Motor (2.4%) and Invesco Asset Management Japan (4.29%) are also listed among the major shareholders. There are no holdings by Korea's National Pension Service.
  • Analyst views: Confirmed target prices range from around 3,300 yen (neutral rating) to 3,767 yen (aggregated by some sites). The current share price already exceeds some targets, so opinions are divided.

Next Checkpoint: Full-Year Results on November 12

The company's full-year results announcement is scheduled for November 12, 2026. There are three points to watch.

  1. Whether the full-year business profit target of 5 billion yen was actually exceeded
  2. What guidance comes out for the next fiscal year (fiscal year ending September 2027)
  3. Whether the growth pace of the existing AI SaaS business, excluding acquired operations, holds up

Because the share price has just renewed its yearly high, expectations may already be largely priced in. It is worth keeping in mind that volatility could increase if results fall short of expectations. This is an estimate, not a confirmed fact.
