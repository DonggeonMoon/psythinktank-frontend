[Today's Stock] Operating Profit Fell Through Q3, Then About ¥20 Billion in Q4 Alone? Kasumigaseki Capital's Last-Minute Reversal

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.11
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

This Post's Topic: A Fourth Quarter Where Everything Landed at Once

Kasumigaseki Capital (霞ヶ関キャピタル, 3498) is a Japanese real estate developer that builds logistics (cold storage) facilities, hotels, and healthcare facilities and sells them to investors. Its results for the fiscal year ended August 2026 came out on October 5, and the market focused less on the numbers themselves than on "how these numbers came about." Results that looked sluggish through the third quarter were booked all at once in the final quarter.

The Full-Year Results

  • Revenue: about ¥163.8 billion (about +69.7% year over year)
  • Operating profit: about ¥27.9 billion (+47.2%)
  • Net profit: about ¥16.7 billion (+62.9%)

The original plan the company announced in October 2025 was revenue of ¥150 billion, operating profit of ¥26.5 billion, and net profit of ¥16.5 billion. The results came in slightly above plan.

What Becomes Clear Against the Nine-Month Figures

The nine-month cumulative figures through the end of May were revenue of about ¥88.5 billion, operating profit of about ¥8.2 billion, and net profit of about ¥4.6 billion. Operating profit had actually declined from the same period a year earlier (about ¥9.4 billion). Simply subtracting these figures from the full-year results (an estimate) implies that about ¥19.7 billion in operating profit and about ¥12.0 billion in net profit were booked in the last three months alone.

PeriodRevenueOperating profit
Nine-month cumulative (end of May)about ¥88.5 billionabout ¥8.2 billion
Full year (FY ended August)about ¥163.8 billionabout ¥27.9 billion
Q4 alone (estimate)about ¥75.0 billionabout ¥19.7 billion

This pattern is typical of a develop-and-sell business model (building properties and selling them to investors). Revenue and profit are recognized all at once when a building is completed and handed over, so quarterly results are uneven and tend to be concentrated at the end of the year. That is why, for this company, it makes more sense to look at the annual figures and at "how much inventory scheduled for delivery next year has built up" rather than at quarterly numbers.

Market Reaction

  • On October 6, right after the results were released, the share price dropped to ¥7,080.
  • After the earnings briefing on October 7, the stock rebounded about +9.9% and closed at ¥7,780. Some interpreted this as easing concerns about a capital increase (issuing new shares, which increases the share count).
  • The October 9 close was ¥7,960 (about +4.5% from the previous day). Compared with the reference price of ¥7,480 on October 2 provided to us, that is a gain of about 6%.
  • This year's high was ¥8,590 on January 16, and the low was ¥5,510 on May 26. The current price has recovered substantially from the low but remains below the high.

Reports also said the company projects ordinary profit to rise about 39% in the next fiscal year, marking a seventh consecutive year of record profit, and brokerages raised their target prices. However, target prices are merely the opinions of brokerages, and posts on internet message boards citing unrealistic target prices cannot be trusted.

Shareholder Composition

The largest shareholder is founder-executive Hiroyuki Ogawa, with about 14.8%. Next come overseas asset managers such as Capital Research Global Investors (about 8.0%) and GMO (Grantham Mayo Van Otterloo, about 6.3%). With such a large share held by foreign institutions, supply and demand in the stock may react sensitively to earnings events. Korea's National Pension Service does not hold the stock.

Key Points to Check

  • Profit concentration: Even a one-quarter slip in delivery timing could shake full-year results.
  • Funding: Development is capital-intensive, so borrowing and capital-increase issues recur. Concerns eased this time, but the structural factors remain.
  • Business diversification: With cold storage, apartment hotels, hospice housing, Dubai real estate, and more, the profit contribution of each business needs to be checked.
  • Volatility: The share price fell about -25% over the past year (data provided as of October 2), and recently it has moved around 10% in a single day.

Summary

These results are a case of "reversing the concerns through Q3 with Q4 deliveries." What to watch going forward is how well the next fiscal year's profit plan is backed by actual delivery schedules, and how the company plans to fund itself.
