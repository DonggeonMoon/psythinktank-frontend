Profits Are Rising While the Stock Retreats: The Gap at Port

Port Inc. (ポート, 7047), listed on the Growth market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange, is known for its job-hunting and career-change site "Career Park." What stands out about this stock lately is that earnings and the share price are moving in opposite directions. The company has been posting record net profits for five straight terms, but its share price has fallen to near its 52-week low.

Note: The most recent price we can confirm is the November 4 closing price. Intraday and closing values for November 5 have not yet been confirmed.

Where Is the Stock Now?

  • It is about 34% below the 52-week high (¥2,769) and only about 3.6% above the low (¥1,756).
  • From the ¥2,100 range in early October (about ¥2,133 on October 6), it has fallen about 15% in a month.
  • P/E shows how many times annual earnings per share the current price represents. A lower number means the stock is cheaper relative to profit, but for growth stocks it can also fall because the market fears profits will turn down.

How Are the Earnings?

  • Fiscal year ended March 2026 (previous year): Revenue of about ¥29.1 billion (about +32.5% year on year) and net profit of about ¥2.71 billion (about +44%), both up.
  • Trailing twelve months: Revenue of about ¥31.6 billion and net profit of about ¥2.79 billion.
  • Company forecast for fiscal year ending March 2027: Revenue of ¥39.0 billion and net profit of ¥3.31 billion (about +22%), pointing to a fifth consecutive record net profit.
  • Q1 (April–June): Net profit of about ¥630 million (+12.3% year on year), reaching about 19% of the full-year plan, similar to the five-year average progress rate (about 18%).

However, forecast revenue grows about 34% while net profit grows about 22%. This means profit margins are not rising as fast as the business is growing, and the market may see that as a burden. This is a fact readable from the plan figures; it cannot be asserted as the cause of the share price decline.

One Growth Pillar: Outsourced Work for Local Governments

According to the first-half analysis material released at the end of 2025, Port saw growing orders related to BPO (business process outsourcing) for local governments, and first-half revenue and operating profit exceeded the company's plan. The number of client municipalities reached 203, up about 14.7% year on year. Moving away from reliance on job advertising and broadening its business base is a positive. On the other hand, when a new business is expanding, staffing and investment costs come first and can make margins volatile.

Shareholder Structure

  • Mr./Ms. Kasuga, known as a founding executive, is the largest shareholder with about 29.2%.
  • Next are Sumitomo Mitsui DS Asset Management at about 5.0%, Maruyama at about 2.8%, Central Tanshi at about 2.4%, and Niinuma at about 2.1% (as of October 1).
  • There was no change in holdings between September 20 and October 1. The large stake held by the top shareholder aligns management and shareholders relatively closely, but the small number of shares in circulation can lead to larger price swings.
  • Korea's National Pension Service holds no shares.

What to Watch Next

  • First-half (April–September) results on November 16 (scheduled): The key is progress against the full-year net profit plan of ¥3.31 billion. We need to see how far the cumulative first-half figure rises from about 19% in Q1.
  • Profit margin: Check whether profit keeps pace with revenue growth.
  • Second-half investment plans: Accelerating investments under the medium-term plan could weigh on short-term profit.
  • Trading volume: The median annual trading value is about ¥240 million, so trading is not very active; watch for price swings when buying or selling large amounts.

Summary

On earnings numbers alone, Port's growth is clear, yet the share price sits near its 52-week low. Whether this gap reflects "excessive pessimism" or "reasonable caution about slowing growth" is likely to be hinted at by the November 16 first-half results. As a small-cap on the Growth market, it is a stock to watch with volatility in mind.
