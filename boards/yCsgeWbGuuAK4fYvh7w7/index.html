[Today's Stock] Revenue Up 3.5x, Yet SBI Global Asset Management's Stock Has Gone Nowhere for a Year — Why?

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.01
조회수 11 views閲覧数 1浏览量 1

Key Summary: Earnings Surge, Share Price Flat

SBI Global Asset Management (4765), listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange, is an asset management and financial information company in the SBI Group. As of October 1, 2026, the share price is confirmed at around 620 yen. According to the data provided, the one-year share price change is -0.79%. Earnings grew sharply over the same period while the stock stayed put, and that is the most interesting thing about this stock.

What Changed: The Group Consolidation Effect

  • Fiscal year ended March 2026: Consolidated revenue was about 27.86 billion yen, up 140.8% from the previous year. Operating profit more than doubled, and net profit attributable to owners of the parent was about 3.07 billion yen, up 86.6%.
  • Total assets: About 53 billion yen, nearly triple the previous level. The inclusion of 11 newly consolidated entities had a large effect.
  • Q1 of fiscal year ending March 2027: Revenue rose about 350.8% year over year, and operating profit rose about 419.1% to roughly 2.7 billion yen. The operating margin rose from 18.0% to 20.8%, and all key quarterly indicators are reportedly the highest ever for a first quarter.

Most of this surge appears to be a scale-up effect from merging asset management affiliates within the SBI Group. That is my own interpretation. It should be kept separate from the same business growing that quickly on its own.

Why Hasn't the Stock Moved? (Speculation)

  • Profit grew less than revenue: The net profit growth rate (86.6%) is lower than the revenue growth rate (140.8%). This means the company got bigger through consolidation, but profitability did not rise at the same pace.
  • No guidance: The company has not disclosed its earnings and dividend forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 2027. Guidance is the company's own forecast of future results, and without it investors find it hard to gauge future profits.
  • Small trading volume: The median annual trading value is only about 150 million yen, which makes it hard for large funds to come in.

Governance: SBI Group Is Effectively the Controlling Shareholder

Among the major shareholders, SBI Asset Management Group holds 43.64%, SBI Financial Services holds 12.61%, and SBI Holdings holds 1.2%. Together that is about 57%, a majority. Restructuring within the group may continue, but the low free float (the share of stock available for public trading) can limit price volatility and trading liquidity. Other holders include Morningstar at 3.07% and Tomohiro Suzuki at 2.03%.

Business Structure

The company operates in two segments. The financial services segment handles data solutions such as seminars, consulting, stock and corporate information, and advertising. The asset management segment provides investment management and advisory services and pension management services. The recent integration has increased the weight of the asset management segment.

What to Watch Next

  • October 27 earnings release: Check whether the operating margin stays in the 20% range in the second-quarter results.
  • Whether guidance is disclosed: If an annual forecast comes out, market uncertainty could ease.
  • Dividend policy: The dividend was raised slightly in the last fiscal year, so it is worth watching whether higher profits translate into dividends.
  • Organic growth after integration: The key question is whether revenue keeps growing after the merger effect fades.
