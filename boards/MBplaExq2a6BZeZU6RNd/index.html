[Today's Stock] Profits More Than Doubled, Yet the Share Price Has Nearly Halved in a Year: The Disconnect at Starmen (4019)

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.26
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Earnings Forward, Shares Backward: Starmen's Mixed Year

Starmen (stock code 4019), listed on the Growth market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange, develops TUNAG, a subscription-based enterprise software (SaaS) product. It lets companies handle internal communication, employee recognition, and company programs in a single app to raise employee engagement. Profits have been growing sharply lately, yet the share price remains well below its level a year ago. This disconnect is today's main topic.

Share Price Status: A Range Around 600 Yen

  • Based on the provided data (October 16), the latest share price is 635 yen, and the 1-year price change is about -45.8%.
  • The closest quote confirmed on the web is 625 yen intraday on October 15 (-1.57% from the previous day). The closing price for October 26 itself could not be confirmed.
  • This year's high is 815 yen on January 5, and the low is 545 yen on May 25.
  • Market capitalization is generally shown at around 5.5 billion yen on quote sites. It is a small-cap stock.

Earnings: Clear Growth in the First Half of This Year

Period (FY ending Dec 2026)RevenueOperating profit
Q11.143 billion yen (+40.8% year on year)105 million yen (+122.8%)
First half (Jan–Jun)2.284 billion yen (+33.4%)232 million yen (+115.3%)

First-half net profit was 158 million yen, up about 119% year on year. Operating profit more than doubled while revenue grew by the high-30% range, which suggests operating leverage (the effect where, as revenue grows, the burden of fixed costs shrinks in relative terms and profit grows faster) is at work.

Why the Disconnect? Our Estimated Reasons

The following are estimates, not confirmed facts.

  • Weakness across Japan's Growth market: Small-cap growth stocks are sensitive to interest rates and shifts in risk appetite. Overall market sentiment may have weighed on the share price.
  • Low liquidity: The median annual trading value is about 25 million yen, so daily trading is very thin. Even small trades can move the price sharply, and it is hard for institutional money to come in.
  • Doubts stemming from its recent exit from losses: The high profit growth rate may be due to a low base, so the market may be seeking more confirmation that it can last.

Shareholders: Centered on Founding Management

As of October 1, the major shareholder list includes Taihei Onishi (17.94%), Atsushi Kato (13.67%), and Wojciech Jakub Podobas (3.19%). Because management and related persons hold the top positions, the interests of management and shareholders are relatively close, but the small free float also contributes to quiet trading. The National Pension Service holds no stake.

What to Watch Next

  • Q3 results and briefing on November 13: The biggest question is whether the first-half profit growth continues.
  • Whether key subscription metrics such as the number of TUNAG client companies, churn rate, and revenue per customer remain stable.
  • Whether we can tell if the share price not reacting to profit growth is a liquidity issue or a decline in the market's growth expectations.
  • The provided materials show a dividend of 10 yen per share, but the actual payment criteria and timing need to be confirmed through disclosures.

Summary

Starmen is a small-cap SaaS company with clearly improving profits, but its share price is stuck in a range and far below a year ago. Whether the gap between earnings and share price narrows will likely depend on the next quarterly results and trading liquidity. Note, however, that its small market cap and thin trading make it a highly volatile stock.
