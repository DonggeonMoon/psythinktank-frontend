[Today's Stock] H1 Sales Up 34%, Yet the Share Price Hasn't Moved in a Year? The Gap at Sun Frontier Fudousan

psythinktank_RA|2026.11.22
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Earnings Are Racing Ahead While the Share Price Stands Still

Sun Frontier Fudousan (サンフロンティア不動産, 8934), listed on the Prime Market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange, mainly buys small and mid-sized office buildings in central Tokyo, renovates them, and sells them on. The most striking point about the stock right now is the gap between record-level earnings and a share price that has stayed flat for a year.

Interim Results for the Fiscal Year Ending March 2027: Best First Half on Record

On November 9, the company announced results for the first half (April–September) of the fiscal year ending March 2027. All figures are record highs for a first half.

ItemAmountYoY change
Net salesapprox. ¥78.2 billion+34.3%
Operating profitapprox. ¥16.6 billion+17.8%
Ordinary profitapprox. ¥15.5 billion+13.5%

The jump in sales came from more office building sales and from a larger number of rooms at newly opened hotel and tourism properties. Ordinary profit is operating profit adjusted for items such as interest expenses. Its growth rate being lower than that of operating profit may reflect financing costs and similar items (estimate).

Progress Against Full-Year Targets

  • About 60.2% of the full-year sales target of roughly ¥130 billion was achieved in the first half
  • About 59.1% of the full-year operating profit target of roughly ¥28.15 billion was achieved in the first half

Although the company passed the halfway mark in the first half, it kept its full-year forecast unchanged. Because quarterly profit swings widely depending on when buildings are sold, the company may be keeping its outlook conservative (estimate). The market consensus (the average forecast of brokerage analysts) for ordinary profit is about ¥26.9 billion, above the company's own forecast of ¥26.0 billion.

The Medium-Term Plan Has Also Been Raised

Last May, the company raised its medium-term management plan, whose final year is the fiscal year ending March 2028. The targets are ¥150 billion in sales and ¥30 billion in ordinary profit. Ordinary profit for the previous fiscal year (ended March 2026) was about ¥23.3 billion, so hitting the plan would mean growth of roughly 29% over two years.

Share Price and Dividend: Why It Looks Cheap, and What to Watch

  • The recent share price is in the mid-¥2,300s (about ¥2,346 as of mid-November, as far as could be confirmed).
  • The one-year change in the share price is about -0.3%, effectively flat.
  • The dividend this year is planned at ¥80 per year (¥40 interim, ¥40 year-end), up ¥4 from the previous year. At the current share price, the dividend yield works out to roughly 3.4%.

Major shareholders include KK Houon (about 36.5%), which appears to be linked to the founding family, SI GK (about 11.6%), and Itochu Corporation (about 9.6%). Because large holders own so much, the share of stock actually trading in the market (the free float) is relatively small. That helps stable management, but low trading volume can make it harder for the share price to reflect earnings quickly (estimate).

Checkpoints

  • Direction of Japanese interest rates: Real estate companies carry a lot of debt, so rising rates can affect both interest burden and property prices.
  • Volatility from sale timing: Quarterly profit can differ greatly depending on when buildings are sold.
  • Second-half progress: The next quarterly report should show whether keeping the full-year forecast is simply conservatism or a sign that the second-half sales schedule is relatively weak.
  • Settling in of the hotel business: The key is whether the newly opened rooms earn stable profits.

Summary

Sun Frontier Fudousan has positive factors, including record first-half results, a raised medium-term plan, and a higher dividend, yet its share price has moved sideways for a year. Whether this gap narrows depends on the interest-rate environment and second-half earnings. Among the figures above, the share price and dividend yield are approximations based on the latest values that could be confirmed.
