[Today's Stock] From 700 Won to the 3,000 Won Range: Can DSM Turn from a Mold Maker into a 'Physical AI Theme Park'?

psythinktank_RA|2026.11.08
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Why a Mold Maker Became a Theme Park Company

DSM (104040) started in 2000 as a maker of auto parts, molds and machine tools, and listed on the KOSDAQ in 2009. In 2025 it merged with Monolith, a leisure platform developer, and this April it changed its name from Daesung Finetech to DSM. Today two business divisions sit inside one company.

  • Monolith Division: operates '9.81 Park', a theme park that combines robotics and AI
  • Mobility Division: auto parts manufacturing based on mold technology, plus solar power and supercapacitor (an energy storage device that charges and discharges electricity in a short time) businesses

Because entertainment and robotics storylines have been layered on top of a traditional manufacturing business, the key variable for the share price is which industry the market decides this company belongs to.

Where Is the Share Price Now?

The most recent price I could confirm is around 3,000 won. The closing price in mid-October was about 3,100 won, and the price in the data provided as of November 2 is 3,215 won. Market capitalization is estimated at around 80 billion won. I could not confirm the exact closing price on the day of writing, so please check again in your brokerage app.

The gap between the 52-week low and high is more than fivefold. That means the stock moves sharply with themes, and with a median trading value of only around 100 million won per day, liquidity is thin. You need to keep in mind that the price can slip when you buy or sell.

Key Watch Point: 9.81 Park Incheon Airport

The biggest event is 9.81 Park Incheon Airport, targeted to open in the first half of 2027. According to reports, construction is roughly 65% complete, and about 87 billion won in investment funding is said to have been secured. The Jeju location has reportedly drawn more than 2.5 million cumulative visitors since opening in 2020, so having operating experience is a strength. Some analysts see the Incheon Airport location and global licensing (lending a brand or technology overseas in exchange for fees) as growth drivers.

In September, the company also held a gravity racing competition in Jeju with a total prize pool of 40 million won, and on December 6 it plans to host the 'GROC 2026 Championship Final' at 9.81 Park Jeju. This reads as an attempt to build brand awareness through content events.

Notable Points in the Shareholder Structure

  • The stake held by largest shareholder Lee Dong-seok and related parties rose from 31.87% on September 21 to 33.54% on October 31.
  • Daesung Emtech and 3 others held 7.44% and Infinitum Partners and 4 others held 5.92%, both unchanged.
  • The National Pension Service is confirmed not to hold shares.

The increase in the largest shareholder's stake is worth noting in terms of management stability. However, the specific reason for the increase should be checked in regulatory filings, and I will not speculate on intent here.

What Investors Should Consider

  • Earnings verification: How much the theme park business actually translates into profit after the merger must be checked in quarterly reports. This research could not confirm the latest quarterly figures.
  • Risk of delays: Large facilities can see construction or opening dates pushed back.
  • Investment burden: Large investments such as the Incheon Airport location bring funding needs and depreciation costs.
  • Volatility: Since the share price rose sharply in a short period, declines can also be large when theme enthusiasm cools.

Summary

DSM is building a new story, 'physical AI entertainment', on top of a stable manufacturing base. The upcoming tests are the opening of the Incheon Airport location in 2027 and the quarterly results released before then. It is a good time to calmly check whether the numbers support the story.
