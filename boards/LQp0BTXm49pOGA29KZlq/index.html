[Today's Stock] Down 46% in a Year, Yet a 4% Dividend Yield? What GMO Internet's ¥10 Billion Nvidia GPU Bet Really Means

psythinktank_RA|2026.09.30
조회수 11 views閲覧数 1浏览量 1

Share price nearly halved, yet earnings actually improved

GMO Internet (4784), listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange (TSE), is a subsidiary about 59% owned by GMO Internet Group that runs internet advertising and develops media (social and app) services. Its recent share price is not in the 500-won range but in the mid-500 yen range. The September 25 close was about 548 yen, and near the close on September 29 it was about 534 yen, down more than 3% in a single day (the September 30 close is hard to confirm as final, so I use the September 29 figure). The share price change versus a year ago is about -46%.

But there is something odd in the numbers. The share price has fallen sharply, yet first-half earnings this year improved.

First-half (January–June) 2026 results

ItemFigure
RevenueAbout 41.3 billion yen (+7.2% year on year)
Operating profitAbout 4.8 billion yen (+46.2%)
Net profit attributable to owners of the parentAbout 3.1 billion yen (+28.1%)
Earnings per share (EPS)10.83 yen

Revenue grew at a single-digit pace, yet operating profit rose by more than 40%. That means profit as a share of revenue has widened. The company is keeping its full-year targets at 82 billion yen in revenue and about 9.46 billion yen in operating profit. First-half operating profit of 4.8 billion yen is slightly more than half of the full-year target.

The most eye-catching variable: a 10 billion yen GPU server

According to reports, the board decided in August to buy Nvidia GPU servers worth about 10 billion yen. A GPU is a graphics processing unit used for AI computation. For a company with a market capitalization of around 160 billion yen, 10 billion yen is by no means a small investment. It could add a new pillar of AI infrastructure to an advertising-centered business, but what revenue this investment will actually generate has not been confirmed yet, and I see it as a point where market hopes and worries are divided.

Dividend and valuation

  • Expected annual dividend: 21.51 yen per share
  • Expected dividend yield: around 4% (about 4.0% calculated at 534 yen)
  • Expected PER (price-to-earnings ratio, the share price as a multiple of earnings): around 25x
  • Market capitalization: around 160 billion yen

The dividend yield alone looks attractive, but it should be noted that the dividend is large relative to earnings. If you simply double the first-half EPS of 10.83 yen to assume full-year EPS (about 21.7 yen), the dividend is almost equal to earnings. This is my own estimate, and the actual payout ratio and whether a special dividend is paid need to be checked in the company's disclosures. It is also reported that the share count increased through a public offering (new share issuance) after the first half, so per-share metrics may be affected by dilution.

Shareholder structure and liquidity

The largest shareholder, GMO Internet Group, holds 59.24%, while a Nomura-affiliated asset manager and a TOPIX exchange-traded fund each hold around 1%, and the National Pension Service holds none. With a high controlling-shareholder stake, the volume traded in the market is limited, but with a median annual trading value of about 700 million yen, trading does take place to a reasonable degree.

Summary: points to check

  • Does the profit improvement continue? Whether operating profit keeps pace with the full-year target in the second half.
  • Results of the GPU investment: When, and through which contracts and revenue, the 10 billion yen comes back.
  • Dividend sustainability: The dividend burden relative to earnings, and capital policy.
  • Why the share price fell: We need to watch the market reaction further to see whether the weakness despite better earnings comes from dilution due to the share issuance, worries about the advertising cycle, or the investment burden.
