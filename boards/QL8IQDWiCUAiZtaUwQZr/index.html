[Today's Stock] Record First-Half Profit, Yet the Stock Is Down 40% From Its Peak: The Mystery of Jade Group's 7x P/E

psythinktank_RA|2026.11.25
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Record earnings, but the stock moved the other way

Jade Group (ジェイドグループ, 3558), listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange, is an online retailer of shoes and fashion. Its first-half results (March–August) for the fiscal year ending February 2027, announced on October 15, 2026, were strong. The share price, however, is not. That gap is the most interesting thing about this stock right now.

The gap in numbers

ItemDetails
Recent share price (11/24 close)¥1,364 (+1.26% from the previous day)
52-week range¥1,324 – ¥2,329 (the high was in late May 2026)
Market capitalizationAbout ¥14.1 billion
P/E ratio (price-to-earnings)About 7.5x
Annual dividend¥30 per share (dividend yield about 2.2%)
First-half ordinary profit¥1.59 billion, +46.2% year on year (a record high)
First-half revenueReported up about 26% year on year

Ordinary profit is operating profit plus non-operating items such as interest and dividends. The company earned ¥1.59 billion in the first half alone, about 61.5% of its full-year plan (¥2.6 billion). According to Japanese media tallies, the five-year average progress rate at this point is around 42%, so this half ran faster than usual. In the June–August quarter alone, ordinary profit rose about 27%.

The P/E ratio is the share price divided by earnings per share, and it is a standard gauge of how cheap a stock is relative to its profits. A P/E in the 7x range is generally on the low side. That said, a low number alone does not prove the stock is undervalued.

Why is the stock weak? Possible reasons

What follows is speculation, not confirmed fact.

  • The full-year target is conservative. The full-year ordinary profit plan first released in May was ¥2.6 billion, only 1–2% above the prior year. Even though 61.5% was already achieved in the first half, no upward revision was confirmed in this announcement. That could mean the company expects second-half profit to fall well below the first half. The market may have read this as a sign that profit is turning down.
  • Seasonality. Fashion e-commerce varies a lot by period. A high first-half progress rate does not necessarily mean the full-year forecast will be raised.
  • Small market cap and low liquidity. With a market cap in the ¥14 billion range and a median annual trading value of about ¥160 million (based on the data provided), even a small number of trades can move the price sharply.
  • Impact of the recent sharp drop. On November 6 the stock closed at ¥1,448 (down about 5.8% from the previous day) and later slid into the ¥1,300s, close to the 52-week low (¥1,324).

How does the company make money?

  • E-commerce business: It sells products from many brands through its LOCONDO.jp store, Rakuten Ichiba, Yahoo! Shopping and others.
  • Platform business: It provides other brands and stores with e-commerce systems, fulfillment outsourcing (e-3PL), and inventory and sales management (LOCOPOS).
  • Brand business: Using the infrastructure above, it runs brands such as MANGO, Vanity Beauty and Bell Florrie.

Because one company owns the storefront, logistics and brands, profit can grow faster than sales once the infrastructure is in place. However, it relies heavily on external marketplaces and is exposed to the consumer economy.

Recent earnings trend

In the previous fiscal year (ended February 2026), revenue rose a little over 1% to about ¥19.4 billion, but net profit grew sharply to about ¥1.57 billion. On a trailing twelve-month basis, revenue is about ¥21.8 billion and net profit about ¥1.93 billion. Profit improvement is far more pronounced than revenue growth, which can be read as a sign that the cost structure has improved. Data sources use slightly different accounting bases, so detailed figures may differ.

Shareholders

The major shareholders most recently confirmed are listed below. Ownership percentages are the same as of September 20 and October 1.

  • Tanaka (Yusuke): 8.85%
  • Baillie Gifford & Co.: 4.98%
  • Asset Value Investors Ltd.: 4.76%
  • Arcus Investment Ltd.: 4.35%
  • Ueda Yagi Tanshi Co., Ltd.: 4.11%

Notably, several overseas asset managers each hold stakes of around 4%. Based on this data, no holding by Korea's National Pension Service is confirmed.

What to watch next

  • Next earnings release: Third-quarter results are scheduled for January 14, 2027. The key questions are whether second-half profit actually declines and whether the full-year plan is raised.
  • Whether the full-year plan is raised: With first-half progress so high, a revision of the conservative plan could change how the market values the stock. Conversely, if a second-half slump is confirmed, the low P/E will turn out to be a price with a reason.
  • Margin sustainability: We need to see whether profit margins hold up as sales grow.
  • Behavior near support: It is also worth watching whether the stock holds near the 52-week low of ¥1,324.

Summary

Jade Group is a classic small cap with "good earnings but a weak share price." The low P/E and a dividend yield above 2% are attractions. On the other side, the conservative full-year plan, small trading volume and sensitivity to consumer spending are burdens. Whether the market has already priced in a second-half profit slowdown, or has simply oversold the stock, will likely be decided by the January results.
