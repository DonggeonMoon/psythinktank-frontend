[Today's Stock] BayCurrent Was Sliding Toward 6,000 Yen Before Earnings. Here Is Why It Has Rebounded 24% in Two Months

관리자|2026.09.30
조회수 44 views閲覧数 4浏览量 4

What Kind of Company Is BayCurrent?

BayCurrent Consulting (6532) is an independent Japanese management consulting firm listed on the Prime Market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange. It operates a single consulting segment and focuses on a core client strategy, working deeply with a small number of large clients. Demand for DX and generative AI implementation support drives its growth, and the stock is a Nikkei 225 component.

For the fiscal year ended February 2026, revenue was 148.332 billion yen (+27.8%) and EBITDA was 52.125 billion yen (+19.9%), for an EBITDA margin of 35.1%. Net profit reached 37.8 billion yen, a record for the 12th straight year.

Q1 Looked Strong, So Why Was the Market Cool?

The first quarter of fiscal 2027 (March to May) looked solid on the surface.

  • Revenue: 44.576 billion yen (+29.9%)

  • EBITDA: 14.888 billion yen (+19.5%)

  • Operating profit: 14.481 billion yen (+18.6%)

  • Quarterly profit: 10.733 billion yen (+19.0%), EPS of 70.99 yen

Gross margin improved to 58.4% from 56.0% a year earlier. The number of consultants grew 19.0% to 5,676, and revenue per consultant rose 5.9%.

The issue was that profit growth lagged revenue growth. Selling, general and administrative expenses jumped 65.0% on hiring, training and upfront investment, and pre-tax profit came in 4.3% below consensus. With expectations for growth stocks running high, that shortfall hit the share price first.

From the July 15 close of 6,383 yen to 7,932 yen on September 18, the stock gained about 24%. Measured from the mid-June low of 5,437 yen, the rebound is roughly 46%.

Factors Behind the Rebound

First, management kept its full-year targets unchanged: revenue of 190 billion yen (+28.1%), EBITDA of 66.5 billion yen (+27.6%), operating profit of 64.8 billion yen (+27.2%) and net profit of 48.1 billion yen (+27.1%). The company also said profit growth should accelerate from the second quarter.

Second, demand is broadening. Beyond DX and AI, the company said new demand has emerged in cybersecurity and defense.

Third, shareholder returns are being strengthened. The annual dividend is planned to rise 30%, from 100 yen to 130 yen, and the company spent 10.4 billion yen on share buybacks in the first quarter alone.

Fourth, the Q1 disappointment was already priced in. The market's focus was on whether the cost increase was temporary, and the unchanged guidance appears to have eased that worry.

How Expensive Is the Valuation?

As of September 18, the forward P/E was 24.2x, P/B was 10.60x and forward ROE was 41.10%. The forward dividend yield was 1.63%. A high ROE supports a high P/B, but it also means the stock could see sharp corrections if growth slows.

Risks to Consider Before Investing

  • If SG&A keeps growing faster than revenue, the margin target could come under pressure.

  • Q1 delivered about 22% of the full-year operating profit target of 64.8 billion yen. The remaining three quarters need to average roughly 16.8 billion yen each, so profit must accelerate from Q2.

  • Concern that generative AI could replace parts of consulting work is a sector-wide variable.

  • As a high-valuation growth stock, it is volatile when rates rise or small and mid-cap growth names are sold. The June drop was largely driven by such supply and demand factors.

What to Watch Next

  • Whether profit growth actually accelerates in Q2 results

  • Consultant headcount growth and utilization (company target range of 80 to 90%)

  • Order growth in cybersecurity and defense

  • Continuation of buybacks and dividends

Summary

BayCurrent combines revenue growth near 30% with high profitability. However, at a P/E of 24x and P/B above 10x, high expectations are already priced in, and if the company cannot prove profit acceleration in the coming quarter, corrections like those in June and July could return. An approach that checks both growth quality and cost control is needed.
