[Today's Stock] Sales Up 22%, Shares Down 60% From the Peak: The Mixed Report Card of Turning Point Brands

psythinktank_RA|2026.10.20
조회수 00 views閲覧数 0浏览量 0

Growth Is Real, So Why Has the Market Turned Away?

Turning Point Brands (NYSE: TPB), known for the rolling paper brand Zig-Zag, is one of the starkest contrasts of the year. Second-quarter sales grew sharply and the company even raised its full-year outlook, yet the share price has fallen to less than half of its all-time high of about $146.90, set in February this year. The latest confirmed closing price is $55.25 on October 13, sitting right next to the 52-week low zone (about $54.69). That is roughly 62% below the peak and about 49% lower than at the start of the year.

Q2 Report Card: Sales Smiled, Profit Cried

ItemQ2 2026
Total net sales$142.9 million (+22.6% year over year)
Modern Oral (nicotine pouch) net sales$68.4 million (+128%), 48% of total net sales
Net income$3.6 million (-75.2%)
Adjusted EBITDA$15.2 million (-50.0%)
Adjusted earnings per share (EPS)$0.23 (below the market estimate of $0.25)

※ EBITDA is earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, a metric used to gauge the cash-generating power of the core business.

The Key Is "Modern Oral" — Nicotine Pouches

Modern Oral products are small pouches that deliver nicotine in the mouth instead of using tobacco leaf. Turning Point Brands competes in this market with its FRE and ALP brands. Q2 Modern Oral gross sales (the amount before deductions such as discounts) rose 149% to $87 million, and its share of total sales climbed to 48%, up from 26% in Q2 last year. This means the company's center of gravity is shifting quickly from rolling papers and chewing tobacco toward pouches.

Accordingly, the company raised its full-year Modern Oral gross sales outlook from $280–300 million to $330–350 million, and set its net sales outlook at $260–270 million. On the other hand, it kept its full-year EBITDA outlook at $70–90 million.

Interpreting Why the Share Price Collapsed

  • Growth costs are eating into profit: The company cited higher spending on sales, marketing and distribution as the reason for lower profit. The shift from the earlier pattern, where profit rose along with sales, is read as a burden.
  • High expectations: Up to the peak in February, expectations for pouch growth appear to have been heavily priced in. This is an estimate; the correction seems to have deepened as profit growth fell short of those expectations.
  • Possible rising competition: The pouch market is one where large players are also investing aggressively, so there may be concern that defending market share will keep costing money (estimate).
  • Dilution: In Q2 the company raised about $59.6 million by issuing shares. New share issuance lowers existing shareholders' ownership percentage.

Financial Strength and Shareholder Returns

At the end of Q2, cash stood at $268.3 million and net debt (debt minus cash) at $31.7 million, so the financial burden is relatively modest. A tariff refund of about $18 million also helped cash flow. The quarterly dividend is $0.08 per share ($0.32 per year), and the dividend yield at the current price is a low 0.6%, so this is a stock driven more by its growth story than by dividends.

The Market's View and What to Watch Next

The Wall Street consensus target price is around $118, well above the current price. However, some firms are lowering their expectations, such as Oppenheimer, which cut its target from $130 to $90. Major shareholders include BlackRock-affiliated funds (about 7.3%), Dividend Street Capital (about 6.3%) and Thrivent (about 6.2%), and no change in their holdings was confirmed between late September and early October.

The next checkpoint is the Q3 earnings release, expected on November 4. There are three points to check.

  • Whether pouch sales growth continues
  • Whether the marketing cost burden relative to sales eases and margins rebound
  • Whether the full-year EBITDA outlook ($70–90 million) is achievable

Whether the gap between sales growth and profitability narrows is likely to decide this stock's direction. Note that the latest price is based on the October 13 close and may have changed since.
